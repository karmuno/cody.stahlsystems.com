Eye on the Bride

Cody Jane Stahl

It’s wedding season, and the bride is so beautiful you can’t help but fall in love with her. When you decide that it’s time to woo her, this is the song you sing.

So go where you’ll go, I’d rather not know. So seal your mouth, I’d sooner head South.
